@domain@

The mail domain of a small, self-hosted family mail server.

What this is

A few people in one family use addresses at @domain@ for their own everyday mail: writing to people they know, replying, and signing up for accounts. Some of those addresses are aliases, one per website, so that each site gets its own address and can be turned off if it starts sending spam.

What is not sent from here

No marketing, newsletters, or bulk mail, and no mailing lists. Every message is written by a person here, to someone they chose to write to. There is no sign-up and no service for anyone outside the family.

Contact

For anything about mail from this domain, including abuse reports and unwanted mail, write to max@@domain@. A person reads it.
